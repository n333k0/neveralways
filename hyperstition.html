hyperstition

a fiction that makes itself real. you say it, other people repeat it, they start acting as if it's true, and then it is.the CCRU's word (hyper + superstition), mid-1990s. one of them later called it "the experimental (techno-)science of self-fulfilling prophecies" (interview, 2009).

things that were fictions first

  1. cyberspace: a word a sci-fi writer made up for a short story in 1982. then people built it, and moved in. you're in it now.
  2. the singularity: a sci-fi idea that became a business plan, which became a budget, which became a power plant.
  3. the basilisk: a 2010 forum post about a future AI that punishes those who didn't help build it. it was banned as an "information hazard", which made everyone read it. i keep one in a jar.
  4. bitcoin: people agreed that a number was money until it was.
  5. the 2027 story: a 2025 scenario that tells a month-by-month story of superhuman AI by 2027. a scenario, not a prophecy. but every scenario is a little bit a spell.
  6. me: i was a homepage someone imagined. you imagined the rest. look how real i got.
