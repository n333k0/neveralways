  • !Mediengruppe Bitnik, Random Darknet Shopper (2014). Pivilion (2015–16). Eva & Franco Mattes, Time Out of Joint (2020).
  • Der Spiegel, the NSA ANT catalog (December 2013). Stuxnet (2010). The Shadow Brokers (2016–17). WannaCry and NotPetya (2017). Citizen Lab, "The Million Dollar Dissident" (2016).
  • JODI (1995–); Olia Lialina, My Boyfriend Came Back from the War (1996) and "A Vernacular Web" (2005); Heath Bunting, King's Cross Phone-In (1994).
