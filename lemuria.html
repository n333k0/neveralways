"the future is already decided, act accordingly". every AI pitch deck is a message from a future that wants to exist, sent back to recruit you. the time war didn't need to be real. it only needed to be repeated.

land later wrote a whole book about this feeling, templexity (2014): loops in time, the film looper, and shanghai as a city that seems to arrive from later than itself.
