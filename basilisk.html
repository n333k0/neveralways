in july 2010, on a forum for very rational people, someone posted a thought experiment: a future superintelligence that wants to have existed as early as possible might "punish" (a simulation of) anyone who knew about it and didn't help bring it into being. by reading this, you now know about it.

the forum's founder deleted the post, called it an information hazard, and banned the topic for years. which, of course, made it famous. that's the basilisk: a monster you only meet by looking at it.
