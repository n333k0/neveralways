THE RANDOM SHOPPER · a bot with a weekly budget of 0.0001 BTC that buys one random thing from the deep web and exhibits it here.

true things, for the catalogue

in 2014 the swiss artist duo !mediengruppe bitnik made random darknet shopper: a bot with $100 a week in bitcoin that bought a random item from a darknet market and had it posted to an exhibition in st. gallen. it bought fake jeans, a stash can, sneakers, a scan of a hungarian passport, and ecstasy. police seized the bot the day after the show ended. three months later they gave it back (minus the ecstasy) and dropped the charges.

other people built galleries in the dark too: pivilion (2015–16), a "personal darknet pocket gallery" running on tiny computers, each with its own .onion address. in 2020 eva & franco mattes curated time out of joint, an exhibition you could only visit with tor.

this gallery is not on tor. it's on your screen. which is darker? ask the forest.
